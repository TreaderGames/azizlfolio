I’m a Unity developer with several years of experience building games across different genres, from multiplayer and sports titles to puzzle and casual games. I’ve worked both on long-term live projects and on rapid prototypes for multiple publishers, which taught me how to adapt quickly and focus on what makes a game fun and viable to ship.

Over time, my role grew beyond just implementing features. I’ve led teams, helped plan realistic sprint goals, and worked closely with management and developers to keep projects moving and solve technical roadblocks when they came up. On the technical side, I’ve worked on multiplayer systems, gameplay mechanics, scalable frameworks and integrations for analytics, ads, and other third-party services.

I enjoy building solid gameplay systems and tools that teams can rely on, and I like being involved in projects from early prototype stages through production though I can reliably adapt to even large existing codebases. My goal is always to keep things practical, maintainable, and focused on delivering a good player experience.
